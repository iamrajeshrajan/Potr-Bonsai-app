# Questions for Potr

Each question below already has a sensible default built in. Answer any where the default is wrong.

## Certificate and traceability
1. **Code format.** Codes currently look like `POTR-B-00001` for bonsai and `POTR-P-00001` for pots. Do
   you want a different prefix or format, for example one that includes the year?
2. **Ownership after sale.** Should the certificate show the buyer's name, or record a transfer of
   ownership? The default records "Sold via potr.in, order #…" with no buyer details, for privacy.
3. **Age.** Age is stored as a start year and labelled *documented* or *estimated*. Do you need
   separate "age of tree" and "years in training" fields?
4. **Private vs public.** Cost, internal notes, nursery location and status changes are private. Each
   care entry has a "show on certificate" switch. Should anything else be hidden or shown?
5. **Signed or PDF certificate.** The certificate is a live web page with a verified history seal. Do
   you also want a printable, signed PDF certificate handed over at sale?

## Shop (WooCommerce on potr.in)
6. **Sold items.** Should sold bonsai stay visible in the shop as "Sold" (default: out of stock but
   still published), or be hidden?
7. **Reserved items.** Should "reserved" hide the item or show it as unavailable? The default shows it
   as unavailable (stock 0).
8. **Pricing.** Are prices tax-inclusive? Do you need GST/HSN details or shipping classes on these
   products (live bonsai are often pickup-only or ship locally)?
9. **Categories and tags.** Are "Bonsai" and "Bonsai Pots" categories enough, or do you want species or
   style as sub-categories or tags?
10. **Pots in listings.** Should a bonsai listing link to its pot's page, and should customers be able
    to buy a bonsai and swap the pot?

## Operations
11. **Staff.** How many people will use the app? Do all staff get full editing rights, or should some
    only log care and photos?
12. **Offline use.** Is there reliable mobile data in the nursery? The current app needs a connection;
    offline logging with sync later can be added.
13. **Reminders.** Scheduled care appears in the app's Care tab. Do you want push or WhatsApp reminders
    when tasks are due?
14. **Existing stock.** Do you have a spreadsheet of existing trees or pots to bulk import?
15. **Card printing.** Is 85 × 55 mm (business-card size) right, or do you want a smaller stake or tag
    that sits in the soil? Will you print in-house or use a print shop?
16. **Domain.** Can we use `bonsai.potr.in` for certificate links? Printed QR codes depend on this
    address, so it should be settled before the first cards are printed.
17. **App distribution.** Is installing the APK directly on staff phones enough, or do you want a
    Google Play listing?
